WOTC-era Base Set, Jungle and Fossil through the newest Scarlet & Violet drops. Japanese sets, promos, trainer gallery hits and full binders of singles.

Every other TCG

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much is admission?
General admission is $5 at the door starting at 9:00 AM. Early buyer admission is $20 and gets you in at 8:00 AM. Kids 12 and under are free with a paying adult.
Do I need a ticket in advance?
No. Admission is cash or card at the door — there's no advance ticket to buy and no line to worry about.
Is this just Pokémon, or other cards too?
Pokémon is the biggest section of the room, but you'll also find One Piece, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Lorcana and a full block of sports card dealers. Vendors list what they sell when they book, and we spread the room out so it's easy to shop.
Can I sell or trade if I'm not a vendor?
Absolutely. Plenty of dealers buy collections on site, and trading across the table is half the fun. If you want to sell in volume, though, grab a table — it usually pays for itself.
Are fake and proxy cards allowed?
No. Counterfeit, proxy and altered cards are not allowed at any table, period. If you're ever unsure about something you bought at the show, bring it to the front table and we'll help you sort it out.
Is there an ATM?
There's an ATM on site, but we'd bring cash anyway. Most dealers also take Venmo, Zelle, PayPal or cards.
Are kids welcome?
Very. Kids 12 and under get in free, plenty of tables run dollar bins, and several dealers hand out free cards to young collectors.
Can I get cards graded at the show?
Most shows have an on-site grading submission table. We announce which grading company is attending on our social pages before each show.
How do I reserve a table?
Fill out the vendor reservation form and we'll confirm availability and send payment details within two business days. Tables are not held until payment is received.
