What you'll find

A real card show, not a flea market

Every table is stocked with cards and collectibles — no candles, no kitchen gadgets. Bring your want list, bring your trade binder, and plan on staying a while.

⚡

Pokémon, vintage to modern

WOTC-era Base Set, Jungle and Fossil through the newest Scarlet & Violet drops. Japanese sets, promos, trainer gallery hits and full binders of singles.

🃏

Every other TCG

One Piece, Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Lorcana, Dragon Ball and more. If people collect it, somebody in the room is selling it.

📦

Sealed product

Booster boxes, ETBs, collection boxes, tins and loose packs — vintage sealed through current retail, priced by people who actually know the market.

🛡️

Graded slabs

PSA, CGC, BGS and TAG slabs across every era, plus raw cards worth sending in. Bring your own and get real offers on them.

🏈

Sports cards too

A solid block of sports dealers every show — baseball, football, basketball and hockey, vintage through today's rookies.

💵

Dollar bins & dig boxes

Dozens of tables run quarter, dollar and dig boxes. The best way to build a set, fill a binder, or hook a kid on the hobby for five bucks.

🔎

Grading drop-off

On-site submission table at most shows — hand your cards over in person and skip the shipping headache.

🤝

Trading welcome

Trades happen across every table, and there's open table space up front for collectors who just want to swap.

🎟️

Door prizes

Free raffle with every paid admission — packs, slabs and sealed product. Drawings run all day and you don't need to be present to win.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much is admission?
Admission is $10 at the door. Kids 10 and under get in free with a paying adult.
Do I need a ticket in advance?
No. Admission is cash or card at the door — there's no advance ticket to buy and no line to worry about.
Where is the show?
The show is in the Sacramento area, right off I-80. The exact hall is being finalized now. We'll post the address here and on @i80card_show as soon as it's locked in, and everyone on the vendor list gets an email the same day.
How big is the show?
50+ tables of dealers. Big enough that you'll find what you're hunting, small enough that you can actually work the whole room in an afternoon.
Is this just Pokémon, or other cards too?
Pokémon is the biggest section of the room, but you'll also find One Piece, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Lorcana and a block of sports card dealers. Vendors tell us what they sell when they sign up, and we spread the room out so it's easy to shop.
Can I sell or trade if I'm not a vendor?
Absolutely. Plenty of dealers buy collections on site, and trading across the table is half the fun. If you want to sell in volume, though, grab a table — it usually pays for itself.
Are fake and proxy cards allowed?
No. Counterfeit, proxy and altered cards are not allowed at any table, period. If you're ever unsure about something you bought at the show, bring it to the front table and we'll help you sort it out.
Should I bring cash?
Bring cash — it moves fastest and gets you the best price. Most dealers also take Venmo, Zelle, PayPal or cards.
Are kids welcome?
Very. Kids 10 and under get in free, plenty of tables run dollar bins, and several dealers hand out free cards to young collectors.
Can I get cards graded at the show?
Most shows have an on-site grading submission table. We announce which grading company is attending on our social pages before each show.
How much is a table, and how do I get one?
Tables are $200 each. Fill out the vendor sign-up form and we'll confirm your spot and send payment details within two business days. Tables are not held until payment is received.
