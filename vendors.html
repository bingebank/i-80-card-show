The fine print

Vendor rules

Short list, seriously enforced — it's what keeps the room good for everyone.

Payment & holds

Tables are $200 each and confirmed when payment clears, not when the form is submitted. We'll send payment instructions with your confirmation email.

Cancellations

More than 14 days out: full refund or credit toward the next show. Inside 14 days: credit only if we can resell your space.

Setup & teardown

Be set up before doors open, and no breaking down before the show closes — early teardown kills the room and costs you a spot at the next show.

What you can sell

Pokémon and other TCGs, sports cards, memorabilia, sealed product, slabs and supplies. Absolutely no counterfeit, proxy, reprinted-as-original or altered cards — this one gets you removed on the spot.

Your space

Keep displays, boxes and racks within your table footprint. Aisles stay clear — the fire marshal is not negotiable on this one.

Conduct

Be straight with buyers and with each other. Misrepresenting an item gets you removed from the show without a refund.

Questions before you sign up?

Call or email and we'll walk you through the floor plan, what's left, and where you'd fit best.
